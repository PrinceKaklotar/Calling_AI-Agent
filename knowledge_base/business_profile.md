## Brand Description

PR Gym is a fictional premium mid-range fitness center in Ahmedabad designed for people who want a clean, well-equipped, supportive, and structured place to exercise.

PR Gym serves beginners, regular gym users, people working toward weight-loss goals, people interested in muscle development, strength-training enthusiasts, senior citizens seeking appropriate fitness support, and customers who prefer guided personal training.

The gym combines independent workouts, trainer assistance, personal training, and scheduled group fitness classes.

## Target Customers

PR Gym is suitable for:

- Complete beginners
- Students
- Working professionals
- Adults interested in general fitness
- Customers working toward weight-loss goals
- Customers working toward muscle gain
- Strength-training enthusiasts
- Customers interested in functional fitness
- Senior citizens who want suitable low-impact exercise
- Customers who want personal trainer support

Customers under 16 years old are not eligible for gym membership.

Customers aged 16–17 may use the gym only with a parent or legal guardian's written consent.

Members aged 60 or above are encouraged to inform the trainer about their fitness level and receive appropriate exercise guidance before beginning unfamiliar exercises.

## What Makes PR Gym Different

PR Gym focuses on helping customers understand how to exercise safely and consistently rather than simply providing access to equipment.

New members receive a basic orientation covering:

- Gym layout
- Equipment basics
- General safety rules
- Warm-up recommendations
- Basic workout guidance
- How to request trainer assistance

Personal training is available separately for customers who want more individualized support.

---

## Beginner Support

Beginners do not need previous gym experience.

A new member can ask the floor trainer for basic assistance with:

- Equipment setup
- Basic exercise technique
- Workout-area navigation
- Warm-up and cool-down guidance
- General exercise questions

Floor-trainer assistance is different from a paid personal-training session.

Personal training provides dedicated one-to-one coaching and is charged separately.

---

## Advanced Training Support

Experienced members can use the strength-training and functional-training areas independently.

Members seeking structured strength or muscle-development programming can purchase personal training.

Trainers can assist with:

- Strength training
- Hypertrophy-focused workouts
- Weight-loss training
- General fitness
- Functional training
- Beginner programs
- Senior-friendly exercise
- Women's fitness

## New Member Joining Process

A new member normally follows these steps:

1. Contact PR Gym or visit the gym.
2. Select an eligible membership plan.
3. Provide required registration details.
4. Complete payment.
5. Review basic gym rules.
6. Receive membership access instructions.
7. Complete the basic orientation.

A customer may also take an eligible trial session before purchasing a membership.

---

## First-Time Visitor Experience

A first-time visitor with a confirmed trial appointment should arrive approximately 10 minutes before the scheduled trial.

The front desk will:

- Confirm the booking
- Verify identification
- Explain basic gym rules
- Provide a short facility orientation
- Explain where changing and locker facilities are located
- Introduce the customer to the appropriate trainer when applicable

## Trial Sessions

PR Gym offers a paid trial session.

**Trial Price:** ₹299

**Trial Duration:** 60 minutes

The trial includes:

- Facility orientation
- Access to the general workout floor during the trial
- Basic equipment introduction
- Basic trainer guidance
- One introductory fitness discussion

The trial does not include:

- A full personal-training session
- A detailed personalized diet plan
- Unlimited gym access
- Group-class access unless specifically stated at booking

A person may take only one trial session.
