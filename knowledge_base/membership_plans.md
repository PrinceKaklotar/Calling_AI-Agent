# PR Gym — Membership Plans

## Membership Overview

PR Gym offers several membership durations.

All standard memberships provide access to the general gym facilities during operating hours.

**Important:** Membership fees do not include personal training unless explicitly stated. Personal training is a separate paid service.

---

## Monthly Membership

**Plan Name:** Monthly Membership

**Duration:** 1 month

**Price:** ₹1,500

**Suitable For:**

- Customers who want short-term flexibility
- Beginners testing a longer-term routine
- Customers who do not want a long commitment

**Included:**

- Cardio equipment
- Strength equipment
- Functional training area
- Locker access
- Changing room
- Shower
- Drinking water
- Wi-Fi
- General floor-trainer assistance
- Eligible group classes included in the membership

**Personal Training:** Not included

**Group Classes:** Included

**Freeze/Pause:** Up to 7 days once during the membership term, subject to approval. No freeze fee.

**Renewal:** Can be renewed at the then-current standard price.

**Upgrade:** Customer can request an upgrade to a longer-duration plan. Any price difference is calculated by the front desk based on the applicable plan rules.

**Downgrade:** A downgrade can be requested at renewal. A current paid membership is not converted to a cheaper plan mid-term.

## Personal Training

Personal training is a separate service and is not included in standard memberships.

Available personal-training packages:

- Single session: ₹700
- 8 sessions: ₹5,000
- 16 sessions: ₹9,200
- 24 sessions: ₹13,200

Personal training provides:

- One-to-one coaching
- Exercise technique guidance
- Workout planning
- Progress monitoring
- Training adjustments

Personal-training packages do not automatically extend the underlying gym membership.

**Duration:** 3 months

**Included:** Standard gym access and included group classes for both members.

## Family Membership

PR Gym does not currently offer a family membership plan.

Customers requiring membership for multiple family members may purchase eligible individual memberships or the available couple membership.

## What Standard Memberships Do Not Include

Standard membership does not include:

- Personal training
- Detailed individualized diet plans
- Merchandise
- Paid special workshops
- Services not listed in the PR Gym knowledge base

---

## Membership Freeze

Freeze eligibility depends on the membership plan.

- Monthly: up to 7 days
- Quarterly: up to 15 days
- Half-Yearly: up to 30 days
- Annual: up to 45 days
- Student: up to 15 days
- Couple: up to 15 days per member

Freeze requests require approval.

The freeze extends the membership expiry date by the approved number of freeze days.

---

## Membership Renewal

Memberships can be renewed before or after expiry.

Renewal is charged at the applicable current price.

Promotional prices are not automatically applied to renewals unless the promotion specifically states that renewals qualify.

---

## Membership Upgrade

A member can ask the front desk to upgrade to a longer-duration membership while the existing membership is active.

The exact amount payable depends on:

- Current plan
- Remaining membership period
- New plan
- Current applicable price

The front desk calculates the upgrade amount.

---

## Membership Downgrade

A current membership cannot be converted mid-term to a cheaper membership with a cash refund.

A customer can choose a cheaper eligible plan at the time of renewal.

---

## Membership Transfer

Memberships are personal and cannot normally be transferred to another person.

Transfer requests may be considered only under exceptional circumstances and require manager approval.

Customers should not assume that a transfer will be approved.

---

## Age Requirements

- Under 16: Not eligible for membership
- 16–17: Eligible only with parent/legal guardian written consent
- 18 and above: Adult membership eligibility
- 60 and above: Eligible, with appropriate exercise guidance recommended

---

## Personal Training and Membership

Buying a personal-training package does not automatically purchase or extend a gym membership.

The customer must maintain an active gym membership to use the gym for personal-training sessions.
