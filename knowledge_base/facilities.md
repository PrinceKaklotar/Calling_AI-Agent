# PR Gym — Facilities

## Facility Overview

PR Gym provides a combination of cardio, strength, functional-training, changing, hygiene, and member-support facilities.

---

## Cardio Area

### Treadmills

PR Gym has multiple commercial-grade treadmills suitable for:

- Walking
- Jogging
- Running
- Incline walking

Treadmills are available during normal gym operating hours.

---

### Cross Trainers

Cross trainers provide low-impact cardio options for customers who prefer less impact than running.

---

### Exercise Bikes

The cardio area includes stationary exercise bikes for:

- Warm-ups
- General cardio
- Low-impact exercise
- Endurance training

---

### Rowing Machines

Rowing machines are available for full-body cardiovascular and conditioning workouts.

Customers unfamiliar with rowing equipment can request basic technique assistance from the floor trainer.

---

## Strength Training Area

### Squat Racks

Squat racks are available for free-weight strength exercises.

Customers should follow gym safety rules and use appropriate weights.

---

### Bench Press

PR Gym has dedicated bench-press equipment for strength training.

---

### Dumbbells

The gym has a range of dumbbells suitable for beginner through advanced training.

Exact maximum dumbbell weight is not specified in the knowledge base.

The AI agent should not invent a maximum weight.

---

### Barbells

Barbells are available for exercises including:

- Squats
- Bench press
- Deadlifts
- Rows
- Overhead pressing

---

### Cable Machines

Cable machines support:

- Upper-body exercises
- Lower-body exercises
- Functional movements
- Isolation exercises

---

### Leg Press

A leg-press machine is available for lower-body strength training.

---

### Smith Machine

A Smith machine is available for guided barbell-style exercises.

---

## Functional Training Area

PR Gym has a dedicated functional-training area.

Equipment includes:

- Battle ropes
- Kettlebells
- Resistance bands
- Functional mats
- Functional training stations

The area can be used for:

- Conditioning
- Mobility work
- Circuit training
- Functional strength
- Group functional training

---

## Group Fitness Area

A dedicated group fitness area is used for:

- Yoga
- Zumba
- HIIT
- Functional Training
- Strength Class

Class availability depends on the weekly timetable.

Some classes have limited capacity and require advance booking.

---

## Locker Facilities

PR Gym provides lockers for members.

Important rules:

- Lockers are for use during the member's gym visit.
- Members should not leave valuables unattended.
- Permanent private lockers are not included.
- Customers should follow the front desk's locker instructions.

---

## Changing Rooms

Separate changing facilities are available for members.

Customers should keep personal belongings secured in the designated locker area.

---

## Showers

Showers are available for members.

Customers should follow cleanliness and usage rules.

---

## Drinking Water

Filtered drinking water is available to members.

Members are encouraged to bring a reusable water bottle.

---

## Wi-Fi

Member Wi-Fi is available inside the gym.

Network credentials are provided at the front desk.

---

## Air Conditioning

The indoor workout areas are air-conditioned.

---

## Cleaning

PR Gym follows a routine cleaning schedule.

Frequently used equipment and common areas are cleaned regularly.

Members should wipe equipment after use when cleaning materials are provided in the workout area.

---

## Music

Background workout music is played in the gym.

Customers should not assume that a particular song or music style will be played.

---

## Parking

On-site parking is available at the building.

Parking availability is subject to available spaces.

PR Gym does not guarantee a reserved parking space for every member.

---

## Equipment Safety

Members should:

- Use equipment according to its intended purpose.
- Ask for assistance if they are unfamiliar with equipment.
- Use appropriate weights.
- Return weights after use.
- Keep workout areas clear.
- Report damaged equipment to staff.
- Follow trainer and staff safety instructions.

---

## Facilities Included in Membership

Standard memberships include access to:

- Cardio area
- Strength-training area
- Functional-training area
- Group fitness area
- Locker facilities
- Changing rooms
- Showers
- Drinking water
- Wi-Fi

Regular scheduled group classes are included.

---

## Facilities Not Available

The following facilities are **not available at PR Gym**:

- Swimming pool
- Sauna
- Steam room
- Childcare facility
- 24-hour access

The AI agent must not claim that any of these facilities are available.

---

## Medical Facilities

PR Gym does not operate a medical clinic or in-house doctor service.

The gym has staff procedures for responding to emergencies, but the AI agent must not claim that PR Gym provides medical diagnosis or treatment.

---

## Accessibility Information

The knowledge base does not specify detailed accessibility features.

If a customer asks about a specific accessibility requirement that is not documented, the AI agent should not guess and should recommend contacting PR Gym directly.
