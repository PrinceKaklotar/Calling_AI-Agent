# PR Gym — Trial Session Policy

## Trial Overview

PR Gym offers a paid introductory trial session for people who want to experience the gym before purchasing a membership.

---

## Trial Price

**Trial Price: ₹299**

The trial fee is paid before the trial session.

---

## Trial Duration

**Trial Duration: 60 minutes**

The trial is a single introductory session.

---

## Trial Eligibility

A person is eligible for one PR Gym trial session if:

- They are at least 16 years old.
- They provide the required identification.
- They have not previously completed a PR Gym trial.
- They have a confirmed trial appointment.

Customers aged 16–17 require written consent from a parent or legal guardian.

Customers under 16 are not eligible.

---

## Number of Trials

Each person is allowed **one trial session**.

A customer cannot repeatedly book trials instead of purchasing a membership.

---

## Trial Booking

Trial sessions require advance booking.

Customers can request a trial by contacting PR Gym.

The customer should provide:

- Full name
- Phone number
- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Basic fitness objective, if they wish to share it

The requested time is not confirmed until PR Gym confirms the appointment.

---

## Trial Arrival

Customers should arrive approximately **10 minutes before** their confirmed trial appointment.

This allows time for:

- Identity verification
- Registration
- Basic rules
- Facility orientation

---

## Identification Requirement

A valid government-issued photo ID is required for trial registration.

The AI agent should not claim that an alternative document is acceptable unless PR Gym confirms it.

---

## What the Trial Includes

The ₹299 trial includes:

- Facility orientation
- General workout-floor access during the trial
- Basic equipment introduction
- Basic trainer guidance
- Introductory fitness discussion

---

## What the Trial Does Not Include

The trial does not include:

- A full personal-training session
- A detailed individualized diet plan
- Unlimited gym access
- Multiple visits
- Guaranteed access to every group class
- A medical assessment
- Medical diagnosis

---

## Trial Timing

Trial appointments are scheduled according to available PR Gym operating hours and staff availability.

A specific trial time is not guaranteed until PR Gym confirms it.

---

## Trial Rescheduling

A customer can request rescheduling by contacting PR Gym at least **4 hours before** the scheduled trial.

Rescheduling depends on available slots.

---

## Trial Cancellation

A customer can cancel a trial at least **4 hours before** the scheduled appointment.

The customer should contact PR Gym customer support or the front desk.

---

## Trial No-Show

If a customer misses the trial without providing at least 4 hours' notice:

- The booking is treated as a no-show.
- The ₹299 trial fee is non-refundable.
- The customer does not receive an automatic replacement trial.

Because only one trial is allowed per person, the customer should contact PR Gym if an exceptional circumstance caused the missed appointment.

---

## Trial-to-Membership Conversion

A customer who purchases a standard membership within **7 days after completing the trial** can have the ₹299 trial fee adjusted against the membership price.

Example:

If the customer completes the ₹299 trial and purchases a ₹1,500 monthly membership within seven days, ₹299 can be adjusted against the ₹1,500 membership price.

The adjustment is applied once.

---

## Trial Fee Adjustment Rules

The ₹299 adjustment:

- Applies only to a standard membership purchase within 7 days of the trial.
- Is not paid as cash.
- Cannot be claimed multiple times.
- Does not apply to an unrelated purchase outside the eligible membership purchase.
- Does not change the published membership price.

---

## Trial and Personal Training

The trial does not include a full personal-training session.

A customer who wants dedicated one-to-one coaching can purchase a personal-training package separately.

Personal-training prices are:

- 1 session: ₹700
- 8 sessions: ₹5,000
- 16 sessions: ₹9,200
- 24 sessions: ₹13,200

An active gym membership is required for ongoing personal-training sessions.

---

## Trial and Group Classes

The trial does not guarantee access to every group class.

If a trial customer wants to attend a group class, availability and eligibility must be confirmed before booking.

---

## Trial Safety

Customers should inform gym staff about relevant exercise limitations before starting the trial.

PR Gym trainers provide fitness guidance but do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

---

## Trial Cancellation and Refund Summary

**Trial price:** ₹299  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Trials per person:** 1  
**Cancellation/rescheduling notice:** At least 4 hours  
**No-show:** ₹299 non-refundable  
**ID:** Government-issued photo ID required  
**Membership adjustment:** ₹299 can be adjusted once if a standard membership is purchased within 7 days after the completed trial.

---

## Trial Policy Boundary

The AI agent must not:

- Promise an extra trial
- Waive the trial fee without authorization
- Guarantee a trial slot before confirmation
- Promise access to an unavailable class
- Promise a personal-training session as part of the trial
- Invent a trial discount
- Diagnose a medical condition
- Change the one-trial-per-person rule
