## Personal Training Details

Personal training includes:

- One-to-one trainer attention
- Exercise technique guidance
- Workout programming
- Progress monitoring
- Training adjustments
- Goal-oriented coaching

Personal-training packages do not include a separate gym membership.

An active gym membership is required for personal-training sessions.

---

## Group Classes

Regular group classes are included in standard memberships.

Included classes:

- Yoga
- Zumba
- HIIT
- Functional Training
- Strength Class

There is no separate charge for regular included group classes.

Certain special workshops or events may have separate pricing if announced by PR Gym.

---

## Fitness Assessment

**Basic Fitness Assessment:** ₹500

The basic assessment may include:

- Basic body measurements
- Weight measurement
- Basic fitness discussion
- General fitness-goal discussion
- Basic exercise-readiness discussion

The basic assessment is not a medical examination.

---

## Diet Consultation

**Diet Consultation:** ₹600 per consultation

The consultation provides general fitness-oriented nutrition guidance.

It is not a substitute for medical nutrition treatment or diagnosis.

---

## Locker

Standard membership includes access to available locker facilities.

A permanent personal locker is not included.

Customers use the gym's designated locker system according to gym rules.

---

## Merchandise

PR Gym merchandise is not included in membership pricing.

Merchandise availability and prices are provided separately by the front desk when stock is available.

The knowledge base does not define fixed merchandise prices.

---

## Discounts

### Student Discount

The student membership is ₹3,600 for three months.

A valid student ID is required.

The student membership price should not be combined with another membership discount unless a promotion explicitly allows it.

---

### Couple Discount

The couple membership is:

**₹7,500 for two people for three months.**

This is the standard couple price.

The couple price cannot be combined with the student membership price.

---

### Annual Membership

The standard annual membership is:

**₹12,000 for 12 months.**

The annual plan already represents PR Gym's standard long-term membership pricing.

---

### Referral Discount

PR Gym offers a referral credit of **₹300**.

Eligibility:

- The existing member must refer a new customer.
- The referred customer must purchase a standard membership of at least three months.
- The referral credit is applied to the existing member's next eligible renewal.
- The referred customer must identify the referring member at registration.

The referral credit is not paid as cash.

Referral credit cannot be combined with another discount unless PR Gym explicitly announces that combination.

---

## Discount Combination Rules

Unless a specific promotion says otherwise:

- Only one membership discount or promotional benefit may be applied to a transaction.
- Student pricing cannot be combined with the couple discount.
- Referral credit cannot be converted to cash.
- Promotional prices cannot automatically be combined with referral credit.
- Staff approval is required for any exceptional discount.

---

## Payment Methods

PR Gym accepts:

- UPI
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- Cash
- Bank transfer

Customers should request a payment receipt after payment.

---

## Receipts

PR Gym provides a payment receipt for membership and service payments.

Customers should retain their receipt for future payment, cancellation, refund, or membership-related queries.

---

## Taxes

The prices in this knowledge base are the standard customer-facing prices.

If applicable taxes or statutory charges are required for a particular transaction, the final payable amount will be communicated before payment.

The AI agent must not invent a separate tax percentage unless that percentage is explicitly available in the current knowledge base.

---

## Refundability of Memberships

Membership purchases are generally non-refundable except where the cancellation policy specifically allows a refund.

Trial fees, personal-training packages, and other services follow their respective policies.

---

## Renewal Pricing

Membership renewal uses the current applicable standard price at the time of renewal.

The original purchase price is not guaranteed for future renewals.

---

## Promotional Pricing

Promotional prices are valid only during the stated promotion period.

PR Gym does not guarantee that a previous promotional price will be available later.

If a customer asks for a promotion that is not listed in the current knowledge base, the agent should say that the current available promotion information is not available.
