# PR Gym — Cancellation, Refund, Freeze, and Transfer Policy

## Policy Purpose

This policy defines the standard rules for membership cancellation, refunds, trial cancellation, membership freezing, membership transfer, no-shows, and complaints.

---

## Membership Cancellation

Members who want to cancel their membership must submit a cancellation request to PR Gym customer support or the front desk.

The request should include:

- Full name
- Registered phone number
- Membership plan
- Membership start date
- Requested cancellation date
- Reason for cancellation, if the customer wishes to provide one

---

## Cancellation Notice Period

A membership cancellation request should be submitted at least **7 days before the requested cancellation date**.

Submitting a request does not automatically guarantee a refund.

Refund eligibility is determined separately under the refund rules.

---

## Cancellation After Membership Starts

Once a membership has started, the membership fee is generally non-refundable.

Exceptions may apply only when specifically allowed by this policy.

Unused days are not automatically refundable.

---

## Cancellation Before Membership Activation

A customer may request cancellation before membership activation.

A refund may be considered if:

- The membership has not yet been activated.
- The request is made at least 24 hours before the planned activation date.

The refund is subject to verification by PR Gym.

---

## Refund Eligibility

A membership refund may be considered when:

- The membership has not started.
- The cancellation request meets the required timing.
- No membership access has been used.
- The transaction can be verified.

Refund approval is handled by PR Gym.

---

## Situations Generally Not Eligible for Refund

Membership fees are generally not refundable when:

- The membership has already started.
- The customer simply changes their mind after starting.
- The customer stops attending the gym.
- The customer does not use their membership.
- The customer misses workouts.
- The customer does not use included group classes.
- The customer fails to request cancellation according to the policy.

---

## Promotional Membership Refunds

Promotional memberships are generally non-refundable.

If a specific promotion contains a different refund rule, that promotion's stated terms apply.

The AI agent must not invent refund terms for an unlisted promotion.

---

## Partial Refunds

PR Gym does not provide standard partial refunds for unused membership days after a membership has started.

Any exceptional refund request must be reviewed by the gym manager.

---

## Trial Cancellation

A trial session costs ₹299.

A customer can cancel or reschedule the trial by contacting PR Gym at least **4 hours before the scheduled trial**.

---

## Trial No-Show

If a customer does not attend the booked trial and does not provide at least 4 hours' notice:

- The trial is treated as a no-show.
- The ₹299 trial fee is not refundable.
- A new trial is subject to PR Gym's one-trial-per-person rule.

---

## Trial Rescheduling

A trial can be rescheduled with at least 4 hours' notice.

Rescheduling is subject to available trial slots.

Only one trial session is allowed per person.

---

## Membership Freeze

Eligible memberships can be frozen once during their membership term.

Freeze allowances:

- Monthly: up to 7 days
- Quarterly: up to 15 days
- Half-Yearly: up to 30 days
- Annual: up to 45 days
- Student: up to 15 days
- Couple: up to 15 days per member

---

## Freeze Conditions

A freeze request:

- Requires approval.
- Must be submitted before the requested freeze begins.
- Cannot exceed the maximum allowance for the membership.
- Extends membership expiry by the approved number of days.
- Does not create a cash refund.

There is no standard freeze fee.

---

## Freeze and Cancellation

A member cannot use a freeze request as an automatic refund mechanism.

If a member wants to cancel, the cancellation policy applies.

---

## Membership Transfer

Memberships are personal.

A membership cannot normally be transferred to another person.

Exceptional transfer requests may be considered by the gym manager.

Customers must not assume that a transfer will be approved.

---

## Personal Training Cancellation

Personal-training sessions should be cancelled or rescheduled at least **4 hours before the scheduled session**.

---

## Personal Training No-Show

If a customer misses a personal-training session without at least 4 hours' notice:

- The session is treated as a no-show.
- The session is deducted from the customer's package.
- The session is not automatically replaced.

---

## Group Class Cancellation

Members should cancel a group-class booking at least **2 hours before the scheduled class** whenever possible.

---

## Group Class No-Show

A missed class without cancellation is treated as a no-show.

Repeated no-shows may result in temporary restrictions on advance class booking.

Regular group classes included in membership are not individually refunded.

---

## Fitness Assessment Cancellation

Customers should contact PR Gym if they need to cancel or reschedule a fitness assessment.

The knowledge base does not define a separate refund guarantee for fitness assessments.

The AI agent should not promise a refund unless the policy explicitly confirms it.

---

## Diet Consultation Cancellation

Customers should contact PR Gym if they need to cancel or reschedule a diet consultation.

The knowledge base does not define a separate automatic refund rule for diet consultations.

---

## Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, PR Gym will process it using the applicable payment/refund procedure.

The exact processing time depends on the payment method and financial institution.

The AI agent should not promise an exact refund-processing duration unless it is explicitly documented.

---

## Complaint and Escalation

If a customer believes a cancellation or refund has been handled incorrectly:

1. Contact the front desk or customer support.
2. Provide the registered name and phone number.
3. Provide membership details.
4. Provide the payment receipt if available.
5. Explain the issue clearly.
6. Request escalation to the gym manager if the issue is not resolved.

---

## AI Agent Policy Boundary

The AI agent may explain this policy but must not:

- Promise an exception
- Guarantee a refund
- Invent a special cancellation rule
- Approve a transfer
- Override a manager's decision
- Invent a refund-processing time

When a situation is not covered by this policy, the agent should explain that the available policy does not specify the requested situation and direct the customer to PR Gym support.
